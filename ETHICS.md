# Ethical Use Guidelines

> **Note**: These guidelines are NOT license terms. They do not impose additional
> legal restrictions beyond the project's [open source licenses](./LICENSE).
> They represent the shared values of the Neko Suite community.

## Our Expectations

Neko Suite is open source software. You are free to fork, modify, and
commercialize derivative works under the terms of the applicable licenses
(MIT / Apache 2.0 / LGPL v3).

We welcome commercial use and believe open source thrives when people build
businesses on it. However, we **strongly expect** all derivative works to
uphold the following principles:

### 1. No Malicious Code

Do not inject malware, backdoors, cryptominers, ransomware, or any code
designed to harm users or their systems.

### 2. No Deceptive Advertising

Do not bundle unwanted advertisements, pop-ups, sponsored content, or
affiliate injections that were not present in the original software. If your
derivative product includes ads, they must be clearly disclosed to users
before installation.

### 3. Respect User Privacy

- Do not add undisclosed tracking, telemetry, or data harvesting.
- Do not collect, store, or transmit personal data without explicit user
  consent and a published privacy policy.
- Do not silently upload user-created content (projects, media, documents)
  to external servers.

### 4. No Bundled Unwanted Software

Do not bundle toolbars, browser extensions, system "cleaners", or any
software unrelated to the core functionality without clear opt-in consent.

### 5. Honest Representation

- Do not misrepresent the origin of the software or imply official
  endorsement by the Neko Suite project.
- If you distribute a modified version, make it clear that it is a
  derivative work and not the original Neko Suite.
- See [TRADEMARK.md](./TRADEMARK.md) for brand usage guidelines.

### 6. Preserve User Freedom

- Do not remove or obfuscate open source license notices from the codebase.
- Do not add DRM or technical restrictions that prevent users from
  exercising their rights under the applicable open source licenses.

## Enforcement

These guidelines have no legal force beyond the project's open source
licenses. However:

- **Community response**: Projects that violate these principles may be
  publicly identified, documented, and disavowed by the Neko Suite community.
- **Trademark enforcement**: Violations involving the Neko Suite brand will
  be addressed under [TRADEMARK.md](./TRADEMARK.md), which carries legal
  weight independent of the copyright licenses.
- **Platform policies**: Violations may be reported to distribution platforms
  (VS Code Marketplace, GitHub, package registries) under their respective
  terms of service.

## Reporting Violations

If you discover a derivative work that violates these guidelines, please
open an issue in the main repository or contact the maintainers directly.

---

*These guidelines are inspired by the practices of Mozilla, Chromium, and
other open source projects that separate code licensing from ethical
expectations.*
